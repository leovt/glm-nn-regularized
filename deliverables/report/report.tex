\begin{abstract}
This project investigates regularized hybrids of generalized linear models and neural networks for insurance claim frequency pricing.
Existing CANN architectures preserve actuarial interpretability by freezing a fitted GLM and training a neural correction, while fully flexible networks may improve fit at the cost of structure.
The proposed model trains the GLM branch and correction network jointly, penalizing the correction parameters so regularization strength controls the trade-off between GLM-like behaviour and nonlinear flexibility.
\end{abstract}
